Offers

Working an offer — from the candidate through to a signed hire on the New hires board (Onboarding → New hires).

Where: Recruiting → Candidates → search their name → click it → Offers tab Also: Recruiting → Offers lists every offer and flags signed offers nobody has moved into onboarding Owner: recruiter / coordinator Before you start: your account role in this app must be Recruiter or Admin (the app has no Coordinator role). No buttons on the Offers tab means you don't have it — ask the Recruiting Manager (Aimee Schaedig). No pay amounts: pay is set in Paycom

What's new: move a candidate into onboarding as soon as the offer is started or sent — don't wait for the signature.

Onboarding one person end to end? Follow the Pre-Onboarding SOP — it already contains these steps in order. This SOP is the offer on its own.

The blue spine is what you do in the app. The dotted-texture box (status) updates itself as you tick. The gold box is done by hand outside the app — the offer letter lives in Paycom.

Part 1

Work the offer

Candidate profile → Applied to tab, then Offers tab

  1. Link the candidate to the job

    Open the candidate's Applied to tab and click Link to a job at the top right of the panel. Type the job title into the Search jobs by title or department box, then click the job in the results.

    If nothing matches, click Create “<your title>” as a new job underneath the search box. That option only appears for Recruiter and Admin — otherwise ask an Admin to create the job first.

    Required: you cannot start an offer until a job is linked. The offer lives on the application, so with no linked job there is nothing on the Offers tab to start.

    In app · candidate

  2. Start the offer, then tick the steps as you go

    On the Offers tab, find the card for the job you are offering and click Start an offer on that card. Six checkboxes appear — tick each as it happens.

    Already showing a status chip other than No offer, with the six steps listed underneath? The offer is already under way and there is no Start an offer link on that card. Go straight to ticking the steps.

    The six offer steps

    Verbal offer extended to candidate› Draft offer letter› Supervisor signs offer letter› President signs offer letter› Offer letter sent to candidate› Candidate signed offer letter

    Paycom is the source of truth for the offer. Every one of these six steps happens in Paycom first. The offer letter template lives there, and the drafting, the supervisor's signature and the president's signature are all done there. Ticking a step here records that it happened — it does not do it. Do the step in Paycom, then come back and tick it. If the two ever disagree, Paycom is right and this app is stale.

    Clicking Start an offer sets the status to Offer planned. From there the status follows the steps: any step ticked = Offer started · Offer letter sent to candidate ticked = Offer sent · Candidate signed offer letter ticked = Offer signed. Clearing every step drops back to Offer planned, not to No offer.

    The dropdown beside the status chip lists all six statuses — No offer, Offer planned, Offer started, Offer sent, Offer signed, Offer declined — and picking any of them sets it. Let the ticks drive it instead; set it by hand only for the two the steps cannot record.

    Offer declined: pick it in the dropdown, type a reason in the box that appears, and click Save.

    In app · candidate
Part 2

Move them in — early

Candidate profile → panel above the tabs

  1. Move to onboarding — before they sign

    Once the status reads Offer started or Offer sent, click Move to onboarding in the panel above the candidate's profile tabs. A form opens — fill in Position, Department, Start date and Company email, then click Move to onboarding again to create the record.

    Company email is already filled in by the house rule — first initial plus last name at skyshare.com, so Hankyu Park is hpark@skyshare.com. Leave it as it is unless an amber warning says someone already has that address; then type a different one.

    No start date yet? Tick the amber checkbox that ends Move them anyway — the second Move to onboarding button stays greyed out until you do, and they sit in the No start date bucket on the New hires dashboard.

    Their candidate record, resume, and interview history stay linked. They appear under Onboarding → New hires, and you land on their onboarding record.

    In app · candidatePer person

  2. If the panel says someone with that name already exists

    Instead of the form you get a gold panel headed Already in onboarding, naming the existing record. Click View that record and check whether it is the same person.

    Same person: click Link to that record. No duplicate is created, and their resume, interviews, and history connect to it.

    Genuinely a different person with the same name: click Different person — create separate. The move form opens underneath with a warning above it — fill it in and click Move to onboarding.

Part 3

One checklist, two views

Offers tab · onboarding record

  1. Keep ticking the offer steps from either side

    Tick them on the candidate's Offers tab, or open Onboarding → New hires, click the person, and tick the same six in the Offer box directly above Checklist. The Offer group inside Checklist is the same six again — ticking them there writes back too. All three doors work; use whichever is in front of you.

    One exception: setting a Checklist offer row to N/A does not write back. Only Done and To do reach the offer.

    Each side updates the other — provided the hire is linked to a candidate with at least one application. A hire added straight to the New hires board with no linked candidate has no Offer box on their onboarding record, and their Offer checklist ticks stay on that checklist only.

Part 4

Signed — the handoff

Offers tab → step list

  1. Mark it signed and set the start date

    When the candidate signs, tick Candidate signed offer letter. The status flips to Offer signed on its own.

    Ticking that step does not prompt you for a start date. Click Add their start date below the step list, type the date, and click Save. That link only appears while no start date is recorded — once one is saved it disappears, and the date shows in the line of dates above.

    Choosing Offer signed in the status dropdown instead asks for the date straight away. The second button in that panel reads Not known yet before the offer is signed, and Cancel once the status is already Offer signed.

    The rest of the onboarding checklist — documents, systems, orientation — carries on from there.

    In app · candidateIn app · onboardingPer person

Why it works this way

An offer lives on the candidate's application, so you can work one for someone you only might hire — no onboarding record needed. The six offer steps are the first six of the 20-step onboarding checklist, which is why editing them on either side keeps both in step.
