Offers

Working an offer — from the candidate through to a signed hire on the onboarding board. An offer is the last act of recruiting; signing is the handoff. The offer and the onboarding checklist are one thing, seen from two places.

Where: Candidate profile → Offers tab Owner: recruiter / coordinator No pay amounts: Paycom owns comp

What's new: move a candidate into onboarding as soon as the offer is under way — you don't wait for the signature. They show on the onboarding board right away, the offer keeps going there, and the six offer steps stay in step whether you tick them on the candidate's Offers tab or on their onboarding record.

Part 1

Work the offer

Candidate → Offers tab

  1. Link the candidate to the job

    On the candidate's Applied to tab, use Link to a job (an offer is always for a specific role). If the job doesn't exist yet, create it right there — no trip out to Jobs.

    In app · candidate

  2. Start the offer, then tick the steps as you go

    On the Offers tab, Start an offer, then check off each step as it happens. The status follows the steps automatically — you don't set it by hand.

    The six offer steps

    Verbal offer› Draft letter› Supervisor signs› President signs› Letter sent› Candidate signs

    Status derives: any step ticked = Started · letter sent = Sent · candidate signed = Signed. "Declined" is set by hand — it's something they do.

    In app · candidate
Part 2

Move them in — early

Candidate → Move to onboarding

  1. Move to onboarding as soon as the offer is under way

    Don't wait for the signature. Once an offer is started or sent, click Move to onboarding on the candidate — they land on the New-hires board so you can track exactly where they are.

    Their candidate record, resume, and interview history stay linked. The offer keeps going on the onboarding record.

    In app · candidatePer person
Part 3

One checklist, two views

Offers tab · onboarding record

  1. Keep ticking the offer steps from either side

    The six offer steps are the front of the onboarding checklist. Tick them on the candidate's Offers tab or on the new-hire's onboarding record — the two stay in step automatically, so nothing is entered twice and nothing drifts.

    In app · candidateIn app · onboarding
Part 4

Signed — the handoff

Offers stepper

  1. Mark it signed and set the start date

    When the candidate signs, tick Candidate signed (or set status to Signed). You'll be asked for the start date — there's a "not known yet" escape so signing is never blocked on it.

    Signing is the handoff: the rest of the onboarding checklist (documents, systems, orientation) carries on from there.

    In app · candidateIn app · onboardingPer person

Why it works this way

An offer is the last act of recruiting, so it lives on the candidate's application — you can work an offer for someone you only might hire, no new-hire record needed. Signing is the handoff to onboarding.

Moving people in early means offer-stage hires are visible on the board instead of hidden until they sign. And because the six offer steps are the same six that open the onboarding checklist, editing them on either side keeps both in step — one source of truth, two windows.
