The blue spine is what you do in the app. The dotted-texture box appears on its own once a hire has an orientation date. The gold boxes are done by hand outside the app — the order goes out through Front, and the after-order handoffs are real-world.

Part 1

Order the cards

App + Front

  1. See who needs a card

    Read the amber banner at the top of the page — N cards to order before orientation. That banner is your order list: only people with an orientation coming up, soonest first. It shows at most 8 names, then a + N more line for the rest.

    Order for the people in the banner. Do not order from the Needs cards tab — that tab, and the Show needed link in the banner that opens it, lists every person still marked Needed, which is most of the company, with no orientation deadline applied. Use it to look someone up, not to decide who to order for.

    Each card shows an amber Order by date — one week before that person's orientation, the last day an order still arrives in time. It turns into a red Order now once that date has passed. The date only appears once they have an orientation date on their record. Order about 2 weeks before orientation.

    In appPer batch

  2. Clear any missing emails first

    Open the Missing email tab. For each person, click the external-link icon at the top right of their card to open their onboarding record. In the Details panel, type their address into the SkyShare email field — first initial + last name @skyshare.com (Hankyu Park → hpark@skyshare.com) — and click Save. Reload the Business cards page before you order.

    If that address already belongs to someone else, ask the Recruiting Manager (Aimee Schaedig), the onboarding team, or the new hire's supervisor which address to use. Do not invent one.

    In appPer person

  3. Select the cards and copy them

    Tick the checkbox at the top left of each card you are ordering. These are cards, not people — anyone with a second card shows an extra tile with a label such as Recruiting, and each tile you tick is ordered separately.

    Check that the gold button now reads Copy N for printer and that N matches the number you ticked. If it still reads Copy all for printer, nothing is ticked and clicking it copies every card currently listed. Then click Copy N for printer.

    The cards paste in SkyShare's card formatting — red field labels, grey title, black values, one block per card. Phone, email and web paste with no hyperlinks.

    In appPer batch

  4. Send the order from Front

    In Front, open the recruiting@skyshare.com inbox and find the most recent thread with Sun Print Solutions (mark.bengtzen@sunlitho.net). Click Reply, paste with Ctrl+V, type the quantity under each name — 100 cards per person — and send.

    No prior thread? Compose a new message to that same address, subject SkyShare business card order — [month and year], paste, and send.

    Front is a separate login — ask your manager for access before your first order.

    FrontPer batch

  5. Mark them Ordered

    Back on the Business cards page, keep the same people ticked and click Ordered in the Mark: row of the bar above the cards. They move to the Ordered tab. To change one person on their own, use the status dropdown at the bottom of their card.

    In appPer batch
Part 2

After you send the order

Front · Ramp · SLC · orientation

  1. Approve the proof

    The printer emails a proof. Check every card on it against the app: name spelling, title, the SkyOps number, the mobile number, the @skyshare.com email and the web address. If all of them match, click the approval link in the printer's email. If anything is wrong, reply with the corrections and wait for a new proof — do not approve.

    Front / emailPer order

  2. Pay the invoice

    Pay the printer's invoice in Ramp (SkyShare's corporate-card and expense tool, ramp.com). No Ramp login or spend approval? Forward the invoice to the Recruiting Manager (Aimee Schaedig) or any other recruiter — do not sit on it.

    RampPer order

  3. Tell Brett the box is coming

    Email Brett Nielson in Marketing (brett@skyshare.com) and tell him a box of cards from Sun Print Solutions is on its way to the Salt Lake City (SLC) office. He receives the box, inspects the cards, and delivers them to Morgan Langholf, EA to the CEO.

    SLC officePer order

  4. Arrange orientation-day availability

    Email Morgan Langholf at mlangholf@skyshare.com and ask her to set the printed cards out on orientation day. Confirm with her the day before orientation.

    MorganPer order

  5. Mark the employee received their cards

    Once a person has their cards in hand, set their status to Received on the Business cards page.

    Anyone based outside SLC — open their onboarding record from the external-link icon on their card and read Job location in the Details panel — needs their cards shipped first. Do not ship them yourself — ask Morgan Langholf (mlangholf@skyshare.com) or Hannah Byers (hbyers@skyshare.com) to ship them, then mark Received once they confirm the cards arrived.

    In appShippingPer person

What the app tracks today

The app tracks the card status per person: Needed Ordered Received Not needed — one status per person. Each status has its own tab, named after the status except Needed, whose tab is labelled Needs cards. A card moves tabs the moment you change its status. Set someone to Not needed when they are not getting cards.

Setting their Order business card checklist item to N/A on their onboarding record (Onboarding → New hires, then click their name) does the same automatically, and setting that item back to To do or Done returns them to Needed. Neither ever changes a card already Ordered or Received.

The Part 2 steps (approve, pay, SLC receipt, orientation hand-off, remote shipping) are manual — this SOP is the source of truth for them.
