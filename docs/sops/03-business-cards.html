People Ops · Standard Operating Procedure

Business Cards

Ordering & fulfillment — from an auto-built card to a printed set in the employee's hand. Cards are built from each person's record; you order in bulk ahead of orientation.

Where: People → Business cards Lead time: order ~2 weeks before orientation Owner: you send the printer order from Front
Part 1

Order the cards

App + Front

  1. See who needs a card

    On the Business cards page, use New hires and Needs cards. Cards are due ~2 weeks before orientation — watch the amber order-by date, and the "Order now" flag if it's overdue.

    In appPer batch

  2. Clear any missing emails first

    Check the Missing email tab. No card goes to the printer without a company @skyshare.com email — fix it on the person's profile before ordering.

    In app

  3. Select and Copy for printer

    Tick the people you're ordering, then Copy for printer. The formatted cards go on your clipboard as plain text — phone, email, and web come across with no hyperlinks.

    In appPer batch

  4. Send the order from Front

    Paste into the printer order email in Front and send. It arrives as clean black text, ready for the printer to lay out.

    FrontPer batch

  5. Mark them Ordered

    Back in the app, set each ordered card's status to Ordered. They move to the Ordered tab so you always know what's out at the printer.

    In app
Part 2

After you send the order

Front · Ramp · SLC · orientation

  1. Approve the proof

    The printer sends a proof — approve it via the email link they provide.

    Front / emailPer order

  2. Pay the invoice

    Pay the printer's invoice in Ramp.

    RampPer order

  3. Confirm SLC received the box

    Check that the SLC office received the shipment of printed cards.

    SLC officePer order

  4. Arrange orientation-day availability

    Make sure the cards are out and available on orientation day — usually coordinated with Morgan.

    MorganPer order

  5. Mark the employee received their cards

    Once a person has their cards in hand, set their status to Received in the app.

    In appPer person

  6. Ship to remote employees

    For anyone not based in SLC, ship their cards to them, then mark Received once delivered.

    ShippingPer person

What the app tracks today

The app currently tracks the card status per person: Needed Ordered Received Not needed — each with its own tab, and a card moves tabs the moment you change its status.

The Part 2 steps (approve, pay, SLC receipt, orientation hand-off, remote shipping) are manual for now — this SOP is the source of truth for them. In-app tracking of the after-order steps is a planned follow-up once the flow settles over a few real orders.
