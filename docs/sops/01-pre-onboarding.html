Pre-Onboarding a new hire, start to finish

Everything starts on the candidate's profile and carries onto their onboarding record — the offer travels with them, so nothing is re-entered. Follow the steps below; the callouts tell you exactly what you'll see so nothing looks broken.

🔑

Before you start: your account must be Admin or Recruiter. As a Viewer the action buttons are hidden. Check under Settings → Team Members; change a role there if needed. (Recruiters can now also create jobs.)

✨

What changed: you now move a new hire onto the board as soon as the offer is under way — you don't wait for the signature. The six offer steps stay in step whether you tick them on the candidate's Offers tab or on their onboarding record, so you can track exactly where everyone is while the offer is still in flight.

The eight steps

People → New hires

Start from the candidate

Open People → New hires and click Start from a candidate. This is always the right first move — it keeps the person's resume, offer, and history attached.

You'll see: the Candidates list with a banner, "Onboarding someone? Find and open their candidate…" That banner confirms you're on the right path.
Candidates

Find them — or add them

Search the list for the person. Already there? Skip to step 3. New to the system? Click New candidate (top right) and fill in name + email/phone.

Watch for: if their email or phone already exists, you'll get a note — "Matched an existing candidate — opened their record instead of creating a duplicate." That's expected, not an error. Just check it's the right person.
Candidates

Open their profile

Click the person's name to open their candidate profile. The offer is worked here; from "Move to onboarding" on, it continues on their onboarding record.

Profile → Applied to

Link them to the job

Open the Applied to tab and click Link to a job. Search the role and pick it. Doesn't exist yet? Type the title and choose "Create … as a new job" right there — no trip to the Jobs page. (You can fill in the job's details later.)

Why this matters: an offer is always made for a specific job, so the offer can't start until a job is linked.
Profile → Offers

Start the offer

Open the Offers tab and click Start an offer. Tick the 6 steps as they actually happen: verbal → draft → supervisor approval → president approval → letter sent → signed. The status follows the steps — you don't set it by hand.

You don't need "signed" to keep going. As soon as the offer is started or sent, move to the next step — you'll finish the signature on the onboarding side.
Profile → Move panel

Move to onboarding — early

Don't wait for the signature. Once the offer is under way, the panel turns gold and pushes you to Move to onboarding. Confirm position, department, and start date. The company email auto-fills (first initial + last name); a warning shows if it collides with someone. They land on the board with the offer still in flight.

No start date yet? Tick the box that sits them in the "No start date yet" bucket — a deliberate choice so nobody quietly disappears from the dashboard.
New-hire record

Finish the offer + work the checklist

You land on the new-hire record. The 6 offer steps are the front of the 19-step checklist and stay in sync with the candidate's Offers tab — tick from either side. Mark Signed and set the start date when it happens, then work pilot documents and onboarding & systems, starting with the "Start Your Onboarding Journey" email via Front.

🔄
Two steps tick themselves — don't do them by hand: "Attended orientation" ticks when you mark the orientation session complete, and "Travel complete" ticks when a trip is booked. If they're already green, that's why.
New-hire record

Mark them onboarded

Once the checklist is done, click Mark onboarded at the top of the hire's record. They move to Post-onboard, where their 30 / 60 / 90-day check-ins live.

Doing several at once? The Checklist tab (People → New hires) lets you tick multiple people and a bulk Mark onboarded appears in the action bar.
🗂️
What happens after: when all their 30/60/90-day check-ins are ticked, they auto-move to Archived as a current employee — the Archived tab's Current / Former filter keeps them separate from people who've left.
🚫
Don't use these for a live hire: the + Add new hire and Import hires buttons on the New hires page create a record with no candidate link — no resume, no offer history. They're only for a true walk-in with no candidate record, or a back-catalog import. Always start from the candidate.

Testing this end to end

Run it, then reset — cleanly

  1. Tag your test person TEST when you add them (the Tags field on "New candidate"). This is what lets you delete them afterward.
  2. Run all eight steps above with that candidate — a fake name is fine, e.g. "ZZ Test Pilot."
  3. Reset when done: open their profile → Delete test (only appears for a TEST-tagged record). Type the name to confirm, and tick "also delete the onboarding record" for a full reset. Real candidates have no TEST tag, so they can't be deleted this way.
  4. Test login accounts: remove them under Settings → Team Members → Remove access (real offboarding) — or use a +test email so the backend test-user delete applies.
