Pre-Onboarding a new hire, start to finish

Everything starts on the candidate's profile and carries onto their onboarding record, so nothing is re-entered.

🔑

Before you start: your account must be Admin or Recruiter. As a Viewer most action buttons are hidden, and the ones still visible (such as New candidate) fail when you save. If your role is Viewer, ask the Recruiting Manager (Aimee Schaedig) to set it to Recruiter under Settings → Team Members. Only Admins can open that page; you cannot change your own role.

The blue spine is what you do in the app, top to bottom. The dotted-texture box is what the system ticks on its own. The gold box is done by hand outside the app — the offer letter lives in Paycom.

The eight steps

Onboarding → New hires

Start from the candidate

In the left sidebar click Onboarding → New hires, then click Start from a candidate → in the top right of the page.

You'll see: the Candidates list, with a gold-bordered banner on a pale blue ground across the top, beginning "Onboarding someone?"
Candidates

Find them — or add them

Type their name into the search box on the Candidates list. Already there? Go to step 3. Not there? Click New candidate (top right), fill in First name and Last name — the form only requires one of the two — add Email or Phone so the app can spot duplicates, then click Create candidate.

Only practising? Type TEST into Tags (comma-separated) before you click Create candidate. Dev and production share one live database, so anyone you create here is a real candidate — and only an Admin can delete them, only on a TEST-tagged record. Full instructions in Testing this end to end at the bottom.
Watch for: if that email or phone already belongs to someone, the box's heading changes to Matched an existing candidate and it tells you it opened that person instead of creating a duplicate. That's expected. Check it's the right person, click Open their record, and go to step 4.
Candidates

Open their profile

Click the person's name in the Candidates list to open their candidate profile.

Profile → Applied to

Link them to the job

Open the Applied to tab and click Link to a job at the top of the panel. Type the job title into the search box and click the job in the results. Doesn't exist yet? Type the full title and click Create "<your title>" as a new job. You can fill in the job's details later. If that option never appears, you do not have job-creating rights — ask an Admin to create the job first.

Required: you can't start an offer until a job is linked.
Profile → Offers

Start the offer

Open the Offers tab. Find the block headed with the job you linked in step 4 and click Start an offer inside that block. Six steps appear: Verbal offer extended to candidate → Draft offer letter → Supervisor signs offer letter → President signs offer letter → Offer letter sent to candidate → Candidate signed offer letter. Don't set the status dropdown by hand; it follows the ticks.

Do the work in Paycom first. Paycom is the source of truth for the offer — the letter template, the drafting and both signatures all live there. Each step here is a record that it already happened in Paycom, so do the step in Paycom, then come back and tick it. If the two disagree, Paycom is right.
Already showing the six steps? The offer has been started already — there is no Start an offer link to click. Go straight to ticking. Judge by the step list, not the status chip: the chip is always there, reading No offer until an offer exists.
Don't wait for the signature. Once you have ticked at least one step, go to step 6. You'll tick Candidate signed offer letter later, on the onboarding record.
Profile → panel above the tabs

Move to onboarding — early

Tick at least one offer step first. The panel above the profile tabs only turns gold once a step is ticked or the letter is sent — a plain white panel means no step is ticked yet. Then:

1. Click Move to onboarding in that panel. A form opens.
2. Fill in Position, Department, Start date and Company email. Company email fills in as first initial + last name; if an amber warning says someone already has that address, type a different one.
3. Click Move to onboarding again — the second button, inside the form — to create the record. You land on it.

No start date yet? Leave Start date blank and tick the amber checkbox that appears under it. The second Move to onboarding button stays greyed out until you tick it. They then sit in the No start date bucket on the dashboard until a date is set.
Onboarding record

Finish the offer + work the checklist

You land on the onboarding record. The 6 offer steps are the first 6 of the 20-step checklist and stay in sync with the candidate's Offers tab — tick from either side. To tick a checklist row, click Done on it; click N/A for a row that doesn't apply to this person. Then, in order:

1. Tick Candidate signed offer letter in the Offer panel when the signed letter comes back.
2. Type the date into Start date in the Details panel and click Save at the top of that panel.
3. Work the Pilot documents group.
4. Work the Onboarding & systems group, starting with Send email on the "Send “Start Your Onboarding Journey” email via Front" row — check the preview, then click Send to …. Do not compose it in Front by hand.
5. The background check is three rows. Tick Run background check in Paycom yourself; Paycom's own emails tick the other two. Check Paycom mail on the New hires page pulls them in now rather than waiting for the overnight run.

6. Put them on an orientation session. Set Orientation in the Details panel, then open Onboarding → Orientation. Find their name in the amber "… still need an orientation" box and pick the session date from the Add to… dropdown beside it. If it says create a session first, click + New session, set Date and Time (MT) and click Create session, then come back to Onboarding → Orientation and use the dropdown. After the session has happened, open it and click Mark complete.

7. Create their trip. Scroll to the Travel & logistics panel at the bottom of this same onboarding record, click New trip and choose Orientation. Fill in the flights, hotel and car. Once everything is booked, set the status dropdown on that trip card to Booked.

Neither of these is optional. Putting them on a session and creating their trip are the only things that can tick Attended orientation and Travel accommodations complete — the last two rows of the checklist. Skip them and those rows sit unticked forever, and nobody arranges the hire's orientation or their flights.
Once "Background check complete — clear to hire" ticks, the person shows on the Dashboard as cleared to hire until you tick Hire in Paycom. That row turns red after 3 days.
🔄
Five steps tick themselves — don't do them by hand: "Send “Start Your Onboarding Journey” email via Front" ticks when you send it from its checklist row; "Candidate submitted background check info" and "Background check complete — clear to hire" tick from Paycom's emails; "Attended orientation" ticks when you click Mark complete on the session they are on; "Travel accommodations complete" ticks when you set their trip to Booked or Completed. Never click Done on these five rows. The last two tick only after you have put them on a session and created their trip — neither happens on its own.
Onboarding record

Mark them onboarded

Once the checklist is done, click Mark onboarded in the button row at the top of their onboarding record — not Archive, and not Mark as former employee. They move to the Post-onboard tab, which holds four check-ins: 30-day, 60-day, 90-day and Benefits enrolled.

Doing several at once? Open the Checklist tab on Onboarding → New hires. The grid is one column per person — tick the checkbox at the top of each person's column, and a bulk Mark onboarded appears in the action bar above the grid.
🗂️
What happens after: when all four Post-onboard check-ins are ticked — 30-day, 60-day, 90-day and Benefits enrolled — they auto-move to Archived as a current employee. On the Archived tab, click Current employees to find them — the other two filters are All and Former (terminated).
🚫
Don't use these for a live hire: the + Add new hire and Import hires buttons on the New hires page create a record with no candidate link — no resume, no offer history. Use them only for someone hired without ever being a candidate here, or when bulk-loading past hires from a spreadsheet. Always start from the candidate.

Testing this end to end

Run it, then reset — cleanly

  1. Tag your test person TEST when you add them — type TEST into the Tags (comma-separated) field on the New candidate box, before you click Create candidate. That tag is the only thing that lets anyone delete them afterwards, and dev and production share one live database, so an untagged practice hire is a real hire.
  2. Run all eight steps above with that candidate — a fake name is fine, e.g. "ZZ Test Pilot."
  3. Reset when done: open their candidate profile and click Delete test — Admins only, and only on a TEST-tagged record, so ask an Admin if you are a Recruiter. In the box that opens, tick "Also delete the onboarding record this candidate produced", type the person's name into Type the name to confirm, and click Delete permanently.
  4. Test login accounts: remove them under Settings → Team Members → Remove access (Admins only). If you create throwaway logins often, sign them up with a +test email so they can be deleted outright.
