SkyShare Talent-Ops · Standard Operating Procedure

Running an orientation, start to finish

Create the session, add the new hires, send the calendar invite and the three emails, then close it out. Two parts stay in your hands — picking a date that works for the execs, and their calendar invite — and the callouts below say exactly where.

🔑

Before you start: your account must be Admin or Recruiter. As a Viewer or a Hiring Manager the buttons that create sessions and send email are hidden. If your role is wrong, ask the Recruiting Manager (Aimee Schaedig) to change it under Settings → Team Members.

✋
You do this part yourself, before anything below. Check the exec team's availability, pick an orientation date that works for them, and pick the time slot they can join. The app does not schedule the execs — you settle the date and time with them first, then build the session around it.

The steps

Onboarding → Orientation

Open the Orientation page

In the sidebar click Onboarding → Orientation. The top of the page lists anyone the system thinks still needs one, under "N hires still need an orientation." The two tabs below are Sessions (what is scheduled) and Cohorts & calendar (hires grouped by the orientation date on their record).

Orientation

Create the session

Click + New session (top right). Enter the Date, and adjust Start (MT) and End (MT) if this one is not the usual 9:30 AM–3:00 PM. Location is pre-filled as SkyShare HQ, Salt Lake City. Add the Address. Leave Google Meet link blank — the calendar invite in step 4 creates the Meet link for you. Click Create session. You land on the session page.

Faster option: on the Cohorts & calendar tab, a group of hires who share an orientation date shows a Create session + add all button — one click makes the session and adds everyone in that group.
Session page → Attendees

Add the attendees

In the Attendees box, open the Add attendee… dropdown and pick a person, then click Add. Repeat for each. The top group, Suggested, is the people who have started or start by this date — normally exactly who you want.

Also here: if you opened the page from the amber "still need an orientation" list, each of those people has an Add to… dropdown that drops them straight onto a session.
Session page → Google Calendar invite

Create the calendar invite

Scroll to Google Calendar invite and click Create calendar invite. This builds the event — title, date, address, and the Meet video link — and invites nobody yet. When it is done the panel shows an Open in Google Calendar link and the Meet link.

🔗
The Meet link the app just made is the same one used at every orientation. You need it for the execs in the next step.
Google Calendar (outside the app)

Send the execs their calendar invite

You do this by hand in Google Calendar. Create the exec team's calendar invite for the time slot they gave you, and copy the Meet link from step 4 into it so the execs join on the same video link as everyone else. Invite the Executives group (FILL IN: confirm the Executives group address — likely level10@skyshare.com), which is the five execs — Cory Bengtzen (CEO), Tommy Aoki, Keith Rabin, Harry Mitchel and Jonathan Schaedig. Then add the same standing guests every time: Mike Newton (SkyOps), Ricky Lee (Safety), Morgan Langholf (EA), and the HR team — Aimee Schaedig, Hannah Byers and Kevin Sherman.

Session page → Google Calendar invite

Invite the attendees to the event

Back in the Google Calendar invite panel, click Add attendees to the invite. Google emails each new hire the calendar invitation. To also invite supervisors or presenters, use Add other guests below it: the attendees' supervisors show as one-click chips (Select all adds every one), and you can search any employee by name or type an email.

Session page → Orientation email

Send the invitation email

Scroll to Orientation email. Tick the checkbox in the header row to select everyone, then in the bar that appears click 1. Invitation. A preview opens showing the recipients and the exact email; review it and click the Send to… button at the bottom (it shows the count). It goes to each new hire's SkyShare email.

Nobody is cc'd on the invitation. Each new hire gets their own copy and no one else is on it. Supervisors are told separately, in the next step.
Session page → Orientation email

Send the supervisors email

With everyone still selected, click 2. Supervisors in the same bar. This sends one email per supervisor, naming all of that supervisor's new hires at once. Review the list of supervisors in the preview and send.

Send this from the select-all bar, not person by person. A supervisor with three new hires gets one email this way; sending it one attendee at a time would give them three.
Session page → Internal summary

Send the internal summary

In the Internal summary box, click Send the summary. This is one email listing everyone who will attend, sent to the internal list: hrotasks@, Morgan, Ricky, Hannah, Kevin, and Aimee. Check the recipients under Who gets the internal summary? if you need to adjust who is on it.

Session page → Schedule the reminder

Turn on the reminder

In Schedule the reminder, tick Send it automatically. The reminder email goes out on its own the calendar day before orientation, to every attendee who has not already had it. You do not send it by hand.

🔄
It always fires the day before, weekends and holidays included, because the email says orientation is tomorrow. Anyone already sent the reminder is skipped, so nobody gets it twice.
Session page → Attendees

Track replies and travel as they come in

As new hires respond to the calendar invite, update their Confirm dropdown from Pending to Confirmed. If someone can't attend, click Move to next → to move them to the next orientation (or the waiting list if none is scheduled). For anyone out of town, set the Travel column to Needed, then arrange it and set it to Arranged — or click +add to book their trip on their record.

Travel varies by who's going. Only out-of-town attendees need it, so handle each as their status lands.
Session page (top)

Mark the orientation complete

After orientation has happened, click Mark complete at the top of the session and confirm. This ticks Attended orientation on every attendee's onboarding checklist in one step.
