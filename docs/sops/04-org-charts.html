People Ops · Standard Operating Procedure

Org charts — adding & linking people

How to put someone on a Crew or Maintenance org-chart card, link them to their profile, and open or close positions.

Where: Fleet → Crew Org Chart / Maintenance Org Chart Access: Admin. No Edit all button at the top and no Edit this aircraft on a card means you don't have it — ask the Recruiting Manager (Aimee Schaedig) Nothing saves until you click Save — except Remove this aircraft while you are editing that one card, which saves at once

Two buttons you cannot undo. Reset to source sits beside Save changes in the Edit all bar: it deletes every saved edit anyone has ever made to that chart and reloads the built-in roster. It exists only in that bar — a card you opened with Edit this aircraft or Edit this location has no Reset to source, so you must be in an Edit all session to reach it. Remove this aircraft sits at the bottom of a crew card while you are editing it, and takes the card's pilots off with it; when you are editing that one card it saves the moment you confirm — there is no Cancel and no undo. Both write straight to the live database the whole company uses. Read the confirmation box before you click OK.

This whole flow is in the app — the blue spine, top to bottom. The dotted-texture boxes are things the system writes the instant you click, before you ever press Save, so they're worth knowing about. Nothing here happens outside the app.

Part 1

Put them on the card

Edit this aircraft / Edit this location, or Edit all

  1. Open the chart

    In the left sidebar click Fleet, then Crew Org Chart or Maintenance Org Chart.

  2. Open the card you need

    Click the card. On the Crew Org Chart each card is one aircraft and opens into the columns Captains, First Officers and, on some aircraft, Cabin. On the Maintenance Org Chart each card is one location (e.g. Parts, Logistics and Base Support) and opens into its role sections.

  3. Turn on editing

    At the bottom of the open card, click Edit this aircraft (crew) or Edit this location (maintenance). To edit several cards in one session instead, click Edit all under Roster at the top of the page.

    To leave a card without changing anyone, click Cancel at the bottom of the card. To leave an Edit all session, click Done in the bar at the top — it reads Cancel once you have changed something, and asks you to confirm before throwing your changes away.

    You are also warned if you try to leave with unsaved changes — reloading, closing the tab, or clicking any link on the page. That includes a pilot's name inside the chart you are editing, which links out to their profile.

    Admin only

  4. Add the person

    Find the column for the seat they are filling — Captains, First Officers or Cabin on the crew chart, the named role section on maintenance. Type their name in that column's Add name… box, pick their status in the dropdown beside it, then click Add. The status is chosen before you add them, not afterwards.

    Crew statuses: On line, In training, Offered, Tentative · external, Tentative · internal. Maintenance statuses: On staff, In training, Candidate, Candidate · internal, Offered.

    If a third dropdown appears listing Fills: … options, pick the named opening this person is taking. Leave it on Adds a new seat only if they are an extra head on top of that opening.

    In app

  5. Change someone's status later

    On the maintenance chart, use the status dropdown on the person's row. On the crew chart there is no row dropdown: a row shows →trng for someone on line and →line for someone in training, and nothing else — any other change means removing them with ✕ and adding them again with the right status.

Part 2

Link them to their profile

the "link" button

  1. Click "link" on their row

    With the card in edit mode, click link on their row.

    In app

  2. Search their last name

    Clear the Search people by name… box — it opens pre-filled with the chart's version of their name — and type their last name, at least 2 letters. Chart names drift from profile names (the chart may say "Augustin" where the profile says "Auggie"), so a first-name search misses.

    Results include candidates and current employees (employees are marked on staff). Click the right person; the row then shows linked.

  3. Fix a link you got wrong

    With the card in edit mode, click linked on their row. Click Unlink from current candidate above the search box, then link them again to the right person. The row goes back to link.

    Unlinking is part of your unsaved changes — it only takes effect when you save the card or the chart.

    In app
Part 3

Openings, moves, and saving

Edit this aircraft / Edit this location, or Edit all

  1. Open or close a position

    Use the + and − buttons on the counter at the bottom of each column — labelled Open seats on the crew chart and Open positions on maintenance. Each open seat shows as a dashed "hiring" slot on the card and adds to the Open reqs count.

  2. Move someone to another card or base

    Click move on their row. In the first dropdown (Move to…) pick the destination — on the crew chart that is an aircraft and a seat, e.g. G200 — First Officer. In the second dropdown pick the status they arrive with. Then click Move: nothing moves until you press it. Their profile link travels with them.

  3. Answer the journey prompt (crew chart, linked people only)

    If a box headed Record this move on … journey? appears, either fill in New role title and Effective date, pick a Transition type, and click Record role change — or click Skip. The chart move stands either way.

    Record role change writes to that person's employee journey straight away, even though the chart itself is still unsaved.

  4. Copy an aircraft to a new tail (crew chart only)

    With the card in edit mode, click Duplicate this aircraft at the bottom of the card. A Copy this aircraft to a new tail panel opens with Aircraft type, Tail number and Owner / company. Fill them in and click Add this tail.

    The seat layout carries over as open seats. Pilots, tags and notes do not — the new tail starts empty. The copy is unsaved until you save.

  5. Save

    Click Save this aircraft (crew) or Save this location (maintenance) at the bottom of the card. In an Edit all session that button reads Save all changes instead, at the bottom of whichever card you have open. The bar at the top of the page has the same thing labelled Save changes. Both save the whole chart — use whichever is in front of you, and prefer the one in the card, because the top bar scrolls out of sight while you work. Nothing is written until you press one.

    Admin only

Worth knowing

Linking is always an explicit pick. The chart never matches by name — two people can share one, and chart names drift from profile names.

Linking a current employee who has no candidate record creates that record and links it for you. Do not go and make a candidate first. It does not put them into recruiting.

That record is created the instant you click the person in the search results — not when you save. It is written straight to the live database the whole company uses, and it stays there even if you then click Cancel and never save the chart. So click the name only once you are sure it is the right person; if you pick the wrong one, use Unlink from current candidate to correct the chart, and tell the Recruiting Manager (Aimee Schaedig) so the stray record can be cleaned up.

The two charts are not identical. Linking and cross-location moves work the same way on both, but the crew chart says On line and Open seats where maintenance says On staff and Open positions; only maintenance lets you change a status from a person's row; and only the crew chart has Duplicate this aircraft and Remove this aircraft and offers to record a move on a linked employee's journey.

Cross-location moves and person-to-profile linking are required on every org chart. That means the crew chart, the maintenance chart, and any department chart added later. If a new chart ships without move and link on a person's row, it is not finished — say so.
