People Ops · Standard Operating Procedure

Org charts — adding & linking people

How to put someone on a Crew or Maintenance org-chart card, link them to their profile, and open or close positions. The same steps work on every chart.

Where: Fleet → Crew / Maintenance Org Chart Access: Admin (Edit roster) Nothing saves until you click Save changes

What changed: the link search now finds current employees as well as candidates. You no longer have to create a candidate record first for someone who already works here — pick them and the link is wired up for you.

Part 1

Put them on the card

Edit roster

  1. Open the chart in edit mode

    Go to Fleet → Crew Org Chart or Maintenance Org Chart and click Edit roster. A banner confirms you're editing; Cancel leaves without changing anyone.

    Admin only

  2. Open the group you want

    Click the card (e.g. Parts, Logistics and Base Support). It opens showing each role section, the people in it, and its open positions.

  3. Add the person

    In the section's Add name… box, type their name and click Add. Then set their status on the row: On staff for a current employee, Offered for someone with an offer out, Candidate for someone still in the running.

    In app
Part 2

Link them to their profile

the "link" button

  1. Click "link" on their row and search their last name

    Search by last name — chart names often differ from profile names (the chart may say "Augustin" where the profile says "Auggie"), so a first-name search frequently misses.

    Results include candidates and current employees (employees are marked on staff). Pick the right person and the row shows linked.

    In app

  2. If they're an employee with no candidate record — nothing extra to do

    Picking them quietly creates and links their profile record behind the scenes. You do not need to go make a candidate first, and it won't put them into recruiting.

Part 3

Openings, moves, and saving

Edit roster

  1. Open or close a position

    Use the Open positions +/− counter in each section. Open seats show as the dashed "hiring" slots on the card and feed the Open reqs count.

  2. Move someone to another card or base

    Click move on their row and choose the destination. Their link travels with them, so you don't re-link after a move.

  3. Save changes

    Click Save changes. Until you do, the banner shows unsaved changes and nothing is written. Reset to source throws away all customisation and returns the chart to its seeded roster — use with care.

    Admin only

Worth knowing

Search last names. The single most common reason "I can't find them" is searching a first name that differs from the profile.

Linking is an explicit pick, never a name match. That's deliberate — two people can share a name, and chart names drift from profile names. The chart will never guess.

Every chart works the same way. Cross-location moves and person-to-profile linking are a standing requirement for the crew chart, the maintenance chart, and any department chart added later.
