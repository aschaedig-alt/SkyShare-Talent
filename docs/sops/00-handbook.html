Chapter 1

Pre-onboarding a new hire, start to finish

The whole journey. Start on the candidate, work the offer, move them onto the New hires board early, then finish the checklist on their onboarding record. Steps 4–7 are covered in more depth in Chapter 2; you do not need to do them twice.

Onboarding → New hires · candidate profile · onboarding record
Before you start: your account must be Admin or Recruiter. As a Viewer or a Hiring Manager — both are read-only — most action buttons are hidden. Ask the Recruiting Manager (Aimee Schaedig) to set your role under Settings → Team Members — only Admins can open that page, so you cannot change it yourself.
Practising? Tag the person TEST in the Tags (comma-separated) field on the New candidate form before anything else. Dev and production share one live database — an untagged practice hire is a real hire, and only a TEST tag lets an Admin delete it. Full teardown steps are in the Pre-Onboarding SOP.

  1. Start from the candidate

    In the sidebar click Onboarding → New hires, then click Start from a candidate → in the top right.

  2. Find them — or add them

    Search the Candidates list. New? Click New candidate, fill in First name, Last name and Email or Phone, then click Create candidate. If you see Matched an existing candidate, check it is the right person and click Open their record.

  3. Open their profile

    Click the person's name in the Candidates list to open their profile.

  4. Link them to the job

    Open the Applied to tab and click Link to a job. Search the title and click the job in the results. If nothing matches, click Create “<your title>” as a new job — fill in its details later. Both Admins and Recruiters can create a job, so if that option does not appear, ask the Recruiting Manager (Aimee Schaedig) to create it first.

  5. Start the offer

    Open the Offers tab. Find the block for the job you just linked and click Start an offer beside its No offer chip. Six checkboxes appear — tick each as it happens. If the steps are already showing, the offer has started and there is no link to click.

  6. Move them in — early

    Don't wait for the signature. Once the status reads Offer started or Offer sent, click Move to onboarding in the panel above the candidate's profile tabs. A form opens: fill in Position, Department, Start date and Company email — the email fills in as first initial + last name, and if an amber warning says someone already has that address, type a different one. Click Move to onboarding again to create the record. No start date yet? Leave Start date blank and tick the amber checkbox that appears; the button stays greyed out until you do, and they sit in the No start date bucket.

  7. Finish the offer + work the checklist

    You land on their onboarding record. Checklist rows are not checkboxes — every row carries three buttons, To do / Done / N/A. To tick a row, click Done on it; click N/A for a row that doesn't apply. The six offer steps are the first six of the 20-step checklist and stay in sync with the Offers tab — tick from either side. Click Done on Candidate signed offer letter, type the date into Start date in the Details panel and click Save. Then work Pilot documents and Onboarding & systems, starting with the Send email button on the “Send “Start Your Onboarding Journey” email via Front” row — that button only opens a preview; click Send to <their address> in the preview to actually send it.

  8. Mark them onboarded

    Click Mark onboarded in the button row at the top of their onboarding record — not Archive, and not Mark as former employee. They move to Post-onboard. When all four items there — 30-day, 60-day, 90-day and Benefits enrolled — are ticked, they auto-archive as a current employee.

Five steps tick themselves — don't tick them by hand: “Send “Start Your Onboarding Journey” email via Front” ticks when you send it with Send email on that checklist row and then Send to <their address> in the preview; “Candidate submitted background check info” and “Background check complete — clear to hire” tick from Paycom's own emails (Check Paycom mail on the New hires page pulls them in on demand); “Attended orientation” ticks when you mark the orientation session complete — under Onboarding → Orientation, add the hire to the session, then open that session and click Mark complete; “Travel accommodations complete” ticks when their trip is marked Booked or Completed — book it under Onboarding → Travel. Only “Run background check in Paycom” is a manual tick. If they're already green, that's why.
Don't use for a live hire: "+ Add new hire" and "Import hires" create a record with no candidate link — no resume, no offer history. Use them only for a walk-in who was never a candidate, or when bulk-importing old records.
Chapter 2

Offers

If you are onboarding one person end to end, follow Chapter 1 — it already contains these steps in order. This chapter is the offer on its own.

Candidates → the person → Offers tab · onboarding record · Recruiting → Offers
Before you start: your role must be Recruiter or Admin. No buttons on the Offers tab means you don't have it — ask the Recruiting Manager (Aimee Schaedig).

  1. Link the candidate to the job

    Open the candidate's Applied to tab and click Link to a job. Type the title in the search box and click the job in the results. If nothing matches, click Create “<your title>” as a new job.

  2. Start the offer and tick the six steps

    On the Offers tab, find the block for that job and click Start an offer. Tick each of the six as it happens: Verbal offer extended to candidate › Draft offer letter › Supervisor signs offer letter › President signs offer letter › Offer letter sent to candidate › Candidate signed offer letter. Ticking a step records that it happened — it does not do it. The offer letter itself lives in Paycom: draft it from the Paycom template and route it there for the supervisor's and the president's signatures, then tick the matching step here.

  3. Watch the status

    Clicking Start an offer sets Offer planned. From there the status updates itself: any step ticked = Offer started, letter sent = Offer sent, signed = Offer signed. Clearing every step drops back to Planned. To record a decline, pick Offer declined from the dropdown beside the status chip, type a reason and click Save.

  4. Move to onboarding — before they sign

    Once the status reads Offer started or Offer sent, click Move to onboarding in the panel above the candidate's profile tabs. Fill in Position, Department, Start date and Company email, then click Move to onboarding again. They appear on the New hires board (Onboarding → New hires).

  5. Keep ticking from either side

    Tick the six on the candidate's Offers tab, or open Onboarding → New hires, click the person, and tick the same six in the Offer box directly above Checklist — use that box, not the Offer group inside Checklist. You never enter them twice.

  6. Signed — the handoff

    Tick Candidate signed offer letter; the status flips to Offer signed on its own. Ticking the step does not prompt for a start date — click Add their start date below the step list, type it and click Save. The rest of the onboarding checklist — documents, systems, orientation — carries on from there.

Sync has one condition: each side updates the other only if the hire is linked to a candidate with at least one application. A hire added straight to the New hires board with no linked candidate has no Offer box, and their OFFER checklist ticks stay on that checklist only.
Paycom is the source of truth for the offer. All six steps happen in Paycom first — the letter template, the drafting, the supervisor's signature and the president's. Ticking a step here records that it happened; it does not do it. Do the step in Paycom, then tick it. If the two disagree, Paycom is right. Pay amounts are set in Paycom and never entered here.
Chapter 3

Business cards

Cards are built from each person's record and ordered in bulk ahead of orientation. The page covers all current staff, so a reorder for an existing employee runs the same way — start from the All staff tab instead of New hires.

Onboarding → Business cards · Front (email) · Ramp (invoices)

  1. See who needs a card

    Read the amber banner at the top of the page — that is your order list: only people with an orientation coming up, soonest first, at most 8 names then a + N more line. Do not order from the Needs cards tab, or the Show needed link that opens it — that tab lists everyone still marked Needed, which is most of the company. Each card shows an amber Order by date — one week before their orientation, the last day an order still arrives in time — or a red Order now flag once that date has passed. The date only appears once they have an orientation date on their record. Place the order about two weeks before orientation.

  2. Clear any missing emails

    Open the Missing email tab. For each person, click the external-link icon on their card to open their onboarding record, type their address into the SkyShare email field (first initial + last name @skyshare.com) and click Save. They drop off this tab. Reload the Business cards page before you order. If that address already belongs to someone else, ask the Recruiting Manager (Aimee Schaedig), the onboarding team or the hire's supervisor which to use — do not invent one.

  3. Select and copy for printer

    Tick the checkbox on each card you are ordering — these are cards, not people, so anyone with a second card has its own checkbox. Check the gold button now reads Copy N for printer with N matching your count; if it still reads Copy all for printer, nothing is ticked and it will copy every card on this tab. Click it.

  4. Send the order from Front

    Open Front, go to the recruiting@skyshare.com inbox and find the most recent thread with the printer, Sun Print Solutions (mark.bengtzen@sunlitho.net). Reply, paste with Ctrl+V, type the quantity under each name — 100 cards per person is the standard — and send. No prior thread? Compose to the printer with the subject "SkyShare business card order — [Month Year]", paste and send. Any recruiter can send this order; the Recruiting Manager (Aimee Schaedig) normally does. Front is a separate login — ask her for access before your first order.

  5. Mark them Ordered

    Back in the app, keep the same people ticked and click Ordered in the Mark: row above the cards. They move to the Ordered tab, so you always know what's out at the printer. To change one person, use the status dropdown at the bottom of their card.

After you send the order — tracked by hand: ① check every card on the printer's proof against the app (name spelling, title, both phone numbers, the @skyshare.com email), then click the approval link in their email — if anything is wrong, reply with corrections and wait for a new proof · ② pay the printer's invoice in Ramp; no Ramp login or spend approval? forward the invoice to the Recruiting Manager (Aimee Schaedig), or any recruiter · ③ email Brett Nielson in Marketing (brett@skyshare.com) that the order is coming — he receives the box at the Salt Lake City (SLC) office, inspects the cards and hands them to Morgan Langholf, EA to the CEO · ④ email Morgan (mlangholf@skyshare.com) to set the cards out on orientation day, and confirm the day before · ⑤ once a person has their cards in hand, set them to Received in the app · ⑥ anyone whose Job location is not SLC: ask Morgan Langholf (mlangholf@skyshare.com) or Hannah Byers (hbyers@skyshare.com) to ship them — do not ship them yourself, and you do not need their address — then mark Received on delivery.
Not getting cards? Set their Order business card checklist item to N/A — that moves them to the Not needed tab, and clearing N/A puts them back to Needed. It never changes a card already Ordered or Received.
Chapter 4

Org charts — adding & linking people

Putting someone on a Crew or Maintenance card, linking them to their profile, and managing openings.

Fleet → Crew / Maintenance Org Chart · Admin only

  1. Open the chart in edit mode

    Go to Fleet → Crew Org Chart or Fleet → Maintenance Org Chart. To change one card, click it and press Edit this aircraft (crew) or Edit this location (maintenance). To change several, press Edit all under Roster in the header. Admin only — if neither button is there, ask the Recruiting Manager (Aimee Schaedig).

  2. Open the card and add the person

    Click the card. On the crew chart each card is one aircraft and opens into Captains, First Officers and sometimes Cabin; on maintenance each card is a location (e.g. Parts, Logistics and Base Support) and opens into its named role sections. Type the name into that section's Add name… box, pick a status in the dropdown beside it, then click Add — status is chosen before you add, not afterwards. Crew: On line, In training, Offered, Tentative · external, Tentative · internal. Maintenance: On staff, In training, Candidate, Candidate · internal, Offered. On the crew chart only, if a third dropdown appears listing Fills: …, pick the named opening this person is taking; leave it on Adds a new seat only if they are an extra head on top of it. Maintenance has no such dropdown.

  3. Link them to their profile

    Click link on their row. Clear the Search people by name… box — it opens pre-filled with the chart's version of the name — and type their last name (2 letters minimum). Pick them; the row shows linked. If they are a current employee with no candidate record, there is nothing extra to do: picking them creates and links their profile, and it does not put them into recruiting.

  4. Open or close a seat

    Use the +/− counter at the bottom of the section — labelled Open seats on crew, Open positions on maintenance.

  5. Move someone to another card or base

    Click move on their row, pick the destination in the first dropdown, pick their arrival status in the second, then click Move — nothing happens until you press it. Their profile link travels with them. On the crew chart, if they are linked to an employee a Record this move on … journey? box appears: fill in New role title and Effective date, pick a Transition type and click Record role change, or click Skip. The chart move stands either way. Record role change writes to that person's employee journey straight away, even though the chart itself is still unsaved.

  6. Save

    Click Save this aircraft / Save this location at the bottom of the card, or Save changes in the edit bar if you used Edit all. Nothing is stored until you do. To leave a single card without changing anyone, press Cancel. In an Edit all session that button in the edit bar reads Done, and switches to Cancel once you have unsaved changes.

Why last names. Chart names often differ from profile names ("Augustin" vs "Auggie"), so first-name searches miss. Linking is always an explicit pick — the chart never matches by name.
The two charts word things differently. Linking and cross-location moves work the same way on both, but crew says On line and Open seats where maintenance says On staff and Open positions; only maintenance lets you change a status from the person's row (on crew a row offers only →trng and →line, so any other change means removing and re-adding them); and only crew can duplicate or remove an aircraft.
Don't press Reset to source. It sits next to Save changes and deletes every saved edit to this chart, reloading the built-in roster. There is no undo. And when you are editing a single card, Remove this aircraft saves the moment you confirm it — there is nothing to cancel.
