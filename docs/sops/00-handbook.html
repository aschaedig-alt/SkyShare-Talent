Chapter 1

Pre-onboarding a new hire, start to finish

The whole journey. Start on the candidate, work the offer, move them onto the board early, then finish the checklist on their onboarding record.

People → New hires · candidate profile · new-hire record

  1. Start from the candidate

    People → New hires → Start from a candidate. This keeps their resume, offer and history attached.

  2. Find them — or add them

    Search the Candidates list. New? New candidate → name + email/phone. A "matched an existing candidate" notice means it found a duplicate — check it's the right person.

  3. Open their profile

    The offer is worked here; from "Move to onboarding" on, it continues on their onboarding record.

  4. Link them to the job

    Applied to tab → Link to a job. If the job doesn't exist yet, type the title and create it right there — fill in its details later.

  5. Start the offer

    Offers tab → Start an offer, then tick the six steps as they happen. The status follows the steps automatically.

  6. Move them in — early

    Don't wait for the signature. Once the offer is started or sent, Move to onboarding. Confirm position, department and start date; the company email auto-fills. No start date yet? Tick the box that puts them in the "No start date yet" bucket.

  7. Finish the offer + work the checklist

    On the new-hire record: the six offer steps are the front of the 19-step checklist and stay in sync with the Offers tab — tick from either side. Mark Signed + start date, then work pilot documents and onboarding & systems.

  8. Mark them onboarded

    Mark onboarded on their record moves them to Post-onboard for their 30/60/90-day check-ins. When those are all done they auto-archive as a current employee.

Two steps tick themselves: "Attended orientation" ticks when you mark the orientation session complete, and "Travel complete" ticks when a trip is booked. If they're already green, that's why.
Don't use for a live hire: "+ Add new hire" and "Import hires" create a record with no candidate link — no resume, no offer history. They're only for a true walk-in or a back-catalogue import.
Chapter 2

Offers

An offer is the last act of recruiting; signing is the handoff. The offer and the onboarding checklist are one thing seen from two places.

Candidate profile → Offers tab · onboarding record

  1. Link the candidate to the job

    An offer is always for a specific role, so link the job first (create it inline if it's new).

  2. Start the offer and tick the six steps

    Verbal offer → draft letter → supervisor signs → president signs → letter sent → candidate signs. Status derives: any step = Started, letter sent = Sent, signed = Signed. "Declined" is set by hand — it's something they do.

  3. Move them in early

    As soon as the offer is started or sent, move them onto the onboarding board. The offer keeps going there and you can see exactly where everyone stands.

  4. Keep ticking from either side

    The six offer steps appear on both the candidate's Offers tab and the new-hire's onboarding record, and stay in step automatically — nothing is entered twice.

  5. Signed — the handoff

    Tick Candidate signed and set the start date ("not known yet" is allowed so signing is never blocked). The rest of the onboarding checklist carries on from there.

No pay amounts, ever. Paycom owns compensation; this app owns the workflow.
Chapter 3

Business cards

Cards are built from each person's record and ordered in bulk ahead of orientation.

People → Business cards · Front · Ramp

  1. See who needs a card

    Use the New hires and Needs cards tabs. Order about two weeks before orientation — watch the amber order-by date.

  2. Clear any missing emails

    Check the Missing email tab. No card goes to the printer without a company @skyshare.com address.

  3. Select and copy for printer

    Tick the people you're ordering, then Copy for printer. The cards land on your clipboard as clean text with no hyperlinks.

  4. Send the order from Front, then mark them Ordered

    Paste into the printer order email in Front and send. Back in the app set each card to Ordered so you know what's out.

After you send the order — currently tracked by hand: ① approve the proof via the printer's email link · ② pay the invoice in Ramp · ③ confirm the SLC office received the box · ④ arrange orientation-day availability (Morgan) · ⑤ mark the employee Received in the app · ⑥ ship to any remote employees.
Chapter 4

Org charts — adding & linking people

Putting someone on a Crew or Maintenance card, linking them to their profile, and managing openings.

Fleet → Crew / Maintenance Org Chart · Admin

  1. Open the chart in edit mode

    Fleet → the chart → Edit roster (admin only). Cancel leaves without changing anyone.

  2. Open the group and add the person

    Click the card, then use Add name… in the right section. Set their status: On staff, Offered, or Candidate.

  3. Link them to their profile

    Click link and search their last name. Results include candidates and current employees (marked "on staff"). Pick them and the row shows linked.

  4. Openings, moves, and save

    Use the Open positions +/− counter for seats, move to send someone to another card or base (their link travels with them), then Save changes.

Search last names. Chart names often differ from profile names ("Augustin" vs "Auggie"), so a first-name search frequently misses. Linking is always an explicit pick — the chart never guesses from a name.
